# MASTER PLAN — Frank's Fairground

> The single source of truth for what this game IS, what ships at v1, and how the rest unfolds.

## The vision (one-line)

*"A rhythm-based competitive eating game in a Belle-Époque Coney Island carnival sitting on a giant hot dog bun, where you fight named celebrity-eater NPCs at a 4-seat table and chipmunks swarm your tray in the final 10 seconds."*

**Tagline:** *"Eat dangerously."*

---

## The 8 vision anchors (research-locked)

1. **Aesthetic:** Frank's Fairground (Belle-Époque) on a giant hot dog bun + food-shaped rides
2. **Core loop:** 90-second contest, 4-seat table, PvE-anchored with seamless live-player drop-in
3. **Skill/grind ratio:** 65/35 — maxed-stat advantage capped at 2.5× (not 6×)
4. **Currency:** Coins + Style Bucks ONLY. Drop Carnival Tickets.
5. **Personality NPCs:** Jaws, Tsunami, MIKI, The Bear + 50+ named characters w/ catchphrases, tells, signature animations
6. **3 secret-sauce mechanics:** Chipmunk window, Foodgasm reaction burst, Pity Pit consolation arena
7. **Progression:** 4 stats × 50 levels + Prestige P1-P5 + 14 unlockable food districts
8. **Retention cadence:** Daily login, weekly format rotation, monthly server-wide boss raid, seasonal events

---

## Refinements (integrated from session)

### Critical (must-haves for v1 launch)
1. **First 60 seconds compressed** — 0:15 to first bite, not 2:00. Spawn-into-seat animation.
2. **Engineered viral clip moment** — Chipmunk window dramatized for TikTok shareability
3. **Mobile UX** — larger green zones on mobile, haptic feedback, two-finger swatting
4. **NPCs remember the player** — per-player state, "back for more, kid?" by 3rd contest
5. **Anti-cheat day 1** — server-authoritative stats, impossible-reaction-time detection, shadow-ban
6. **Discord community live before launch** — 50k member target by Month 3

### Important (do or leave value on table)
7. **Ambient fair life** — crowd cheers, ride music, vendor calls, NPCs walking paths
8. **Pre-contest warm-up window** — 30-sec phase for emotes, NPC trash-talk, hype
9. **Free starter cosmetic** — Frank's Apron at first win, instant identity
10. **"I almost won" loss design** — lose by ≤2 = "SO CLOSE!" +30 coins, lose 6+ = Pity Pit door opens
11. **Light ranked ladder at v1** — weekly leaderboard, top 10 = temporary gold aura
12. **"Tomorrow Reveal" at session end** — next-day hooks visible at logout

### New ideas to add (originated this session)
13. **Coach Mode** — at 50 wins, "Bobby Crumb" NPC asks to apprentice you, becomes follower + small permanent buff
14. **Glory Reel** — 5-sec highlight clip auto-generated per contest, saved to in-game gallery, shareable
15. **"What's That?" mystery door** — locked door in boardwalk, hints weekly, Speakeasy unlocks at Week 4

### Rejected (don't let anyone talk you into these)
- ❌ Premium currency that buys stats
- ❌ Player-to-player trading
- ❌ Real-brand crossovers
